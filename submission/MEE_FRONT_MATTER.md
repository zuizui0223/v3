# Refine before loss: an information-order theory for ecological observation

## Abstract

**1.** Ecological observation systems do more than classify events: they determine which latent ecological worlds remain compatible with the record that survives measurement, row entry and interpretation. We distinguish three recurrent information operations that are often collapsed into one accuracy problem: refinement by retained side information, support loss through record-entry selection, and semantic coarsening of rich retained evidence.

**2.** We formulate these operations with compatible-world fibres and identified sets. Non-destructive augmentation can only refine or preserve a compatible class; opportunities removed before row entry cannot have their latent composition reconstructed from the selected table alone; and deterministic semantic coarsening cannot create distinctions absent from its richer input. Exact finite-world witnesses establish strict examples, while frozen controlled simulations test reference-guided refinement and semantic coarsening.

**3.** External empirical data show that record-entry selection can alter an ecological estimand before downstream classification. Among 881 CCTV-confirmed fox/badger passes, badger representation increased from 0.3598 among true passes to 0.4393 after triggering and 0.4828 after confirmed capture, with the direction retained in three of four camera positions. In protected acoustic data, a true late-minus-early contrast of 0.1308 was essentially absent after upstream selection even under oracle true-entry-only downstream semantics. Entry-aware correction helped within some calibration domains but worsened under a frozen camera-plus-position double holdout.

**4.** Observation quality is therefore stage-specific rather than exhausted by detector accuracy. Monitoring systems should retain justified side information before irreversible loss, audit selection from outside the selection, preserve reversible representations, and delay semantic collapse until the scientific target licenses it. The present claims do not establish universal physical benefit, universal correction transport or field-valid biological absence from low detector support; they define the retained evidence object passed to subsequent identification and experiment-design analyses.
