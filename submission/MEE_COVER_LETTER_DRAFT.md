# Cover letter draft — Methods in Ecology and Evolution

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Refine before loss: an information-order theory for ecological observation,”** as a Standard Research Article in *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*.

The manuscript asks a design-stage question that precedes downstream ecological modelling: how does an observation system change the scientific distinctions preserved in the record before inference begins? We integrate three information operations in one compatible-world framework: refinement by retained side information, support loss through record-entry selection, and semantic coarsening of retained evidence. The structural results are paired with exact finite-world witnesses, controlled synthetic stress tests, and external camera-trap and acoustic analyses.

The empirical record-entry component deliberately retains adverse results. In the position-standardized camera-trap audit, the combined frozen promotion object records `all_frozen_criteria_met = false` because the predeclared otter all-camera criterion was not satisfied. The fox/badger trigger and capture criterion nevertheless remained positive under both registered weighting schemes. We therefore do not present the artifact as a globally promoted success. The manuscript reports the fox/badger result at its supported ceiling and retains the failed otter criterion and the adverse camera-plus-position correction transport result rather than retuning or excluding them. This distinction is recorded in the machine-readable claim and evidence manifest.

The manuscript consolidates components that were previously developed in separate public research repositories (V3, REC and TNOA). Those source repositories are retained as provenance and reproducibility units, not as concurrent standalone submission targets. A cross-manuscript firewall also separates this Observation paper from a distinct Evidence paper concerned with identification boundaries, next-measurement choice and target reportability.

An anonymized reviewer package is being prepared with executable theory and benchmark code, regression tests, machine-readable theorem and claim ledgers, pinned quantitative evidence, deterministic figure-generation scripts and source-provenance records. Final software licensing, author metadata and AI/LLM disclosure will be supplied after author approval. BirdVox-full-night v3.0 is CC BY 4.0; for the Findlay camera-trap data, the open-access article identifies the public GitHub datasets as online resources, and written reuse clarification is being treated as a submission-governance item.

Thank you for considering this work.
